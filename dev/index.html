FlowTransformBindings.jl

Bindings to the transform libraries the flow-analysis packages share, so each library is bound once. Nothing is exported; the public names are declared public and called qualified:

using FlowTransformBindings: FlowTransformBindings as FTB
  • Nonuniform FFTs: FTB.plan_nufft plans the type-1 and type-2 transforms in 1, 2 or 3 dimensions through FINUFFT (cuFINUFFT on CUDA arrays) or NonuniformFFTs.jl, one interface for both.
  • Packed layout: the wavenumber axes of a transformed field, the half a real field's transform stores, the weights that sum it as the full spectrum, the widened axes holding both ±n/2 where the samples tell them apart, and the dealiasing cutoffs and exact padding of pseudospectral products.
  • Least squares: FTB.lsmr! solves stacks of least-squares problems sharing one operator by LSMR, such as a Fourier fit to scattered samples through a NUFFT plan.
  • FastTransforms from tasks: FTB.with_fasttransforms_threads makes FastTransforms calls at a chosen OpenMP thread count, safe from Julia tasks.

The library tags FTB.FINUFFTBackend and FTB.NonuniformFFTsBackend subtype SpectralBackends.AbstractNUFFTSpectralBackend, so a package that selects transforms by SpectralBackends' tags takes them as they are. Each library binding is a package extension, loaded with the library.

FlowTransformBindings.FlowTransformBindings — Module
FlowTransformBindings

Bindings to the transform libraries the flow-analysis packages share. Nothing is exported; callers use qualified names.

  • plan_nufft: nonuniform FFTs in 1, 2 or 3 dimensions through FINUFFTBackend or NonuniformFFTsBackend, with nufft_type1!, nufft_type2!, set_nodes!, close! and task_local_plan.
  • RFFTAxis and FFTAxis: the packed layout of a transformed field, with its fold weights, widened axes, dealiasing and exact padding.
  • lsmr!: least squares by LSMR for stacks of right-hand sides sharing one operator, such as a NUFFTOperator over a plan or a FunctionOperator.
  • with_fasttransforms_threads: FastTransforms calls at a chosen OpenMP thread count, safe to make from Julia tasks. Its methods live in the FastTransforms extension.
